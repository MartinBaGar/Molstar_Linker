FAQ & Troubleshooting

Why doesn’t the badge appear next to my file?

  • The file extension may not be supported.
  • The site may not be fully supported. Try refreshing the page or authorizing a custom domain.
  • The file link may be hidden or loaded dynamically. Wait for the page to fully load.

I get a download error when clicking the badge.

  • The file URL may be too long (max 2048 characters). Shorten the filename or host it elsewhere.

It works on GitHub but not my institutional GitLab.

  • Your institutional GitLab is likely a private instance. Authorize the domain in the extension’s Options tab.

The Mol​* viewer fails to load the structure.

  • The file may be corrupted or in an unsupported format. Verify the file integrity.
  • The extension’s sandbox may have failed to load. Refresh the page or reinstall the extension.

The badge appears but nothing happens when I click it.

  • Site scripts may be blocking the click handler. Disable conflicting extensions or site scripts.
  • The extension context may be invalidated. Refresh the page or restart your browser.
