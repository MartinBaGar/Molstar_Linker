Installation

Table of Contents

  • On your computer
  • On your mobile

This is basically two simple steps. Choose your device and choose your browser.

On your computer

Firefox

  1. Open the Firefox Add-ons page for Mol* Linker.
  2. Click Add to Firefox and confirm the installation.

Chrome, Edge, or Brave

  1. Open the Chrome Web Store page for Mol* Linker.
  2. Click Add to Chrome (or Add to Edge / Add to Brave) and confirm the installation.

The extension will activate automatically. No restart is required.

On your mobile

Firefox

Firefox natively supporting web extensions, you can get Mol* Linker work on your mobile. You can find more information on how to proceed there.
