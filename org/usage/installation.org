#+title: Installation
#+weight: 210

Install Mol* Linker in two simple steps. Choose your browser below:

*Chrome, Edge, or Brave*
1. Open the [[https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/clemcfbjdnmakcllpobdkmhlbobfbomd?utm_source=item-share-cb][Chrome Web Store page for Mol* Linker]].
2. Click *Add to Chrome* (or *Add to Edge* / *Add to Brave*) and confirm the installation.

*Firefox*
1. Open the [[https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/mol-linker/][Firefox Add-ons page for Mol* Linker]].
2. Click *Add to Firefox* and confirm the installation.

The extension will activate automatically. No restart is required.

You are now ready to [[file:usage.org][open structures]] in your browser !
