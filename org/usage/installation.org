#+title: Installation
#+weight: 210

This is basically two simple steps. Choose your device and choose your browser.

** On your computer
*Firefox*
1. Open the [[https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/mol-linker/][Firefox Add-ons page for Mol* Linker]].
2. Click *Add to Firefox* and confirm the installation.

*Chrome, Edge, or Brave*
1. Open the [[https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/clemcfbjdnmakcllpobdkmhlbobfbomd?utm_source=item-share-cb][Chrome Web Store page for Mol* Linker]].
2. Click *Add to Chrome* (or *Add to Edge* / *Add to Brave*) and confirm the installation.

The extension will activate automatically. No restart is required.

** On your mobile
*Firefox*

Firefox natively supporting web extensions, you can get Mol* Linker work on your mobile. You can find more information on how to proceed [[https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/find-and-install-add-ons-firefox-android][there]].

*Chromium-based*

Some non-official chromium implementation support web extensions. Here is a non-exhaustive list of browsers that showed compatibility with Mol* Linker to some extend (no warranties):
- [[https://github.com/jqssun/android-titanium-browser][Titanium Browser]]

You are now ready to [[file:usage.org][open structures]] in your browser !
