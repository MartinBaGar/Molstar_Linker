Welcome to the official documentation for Mol* Linker!

* About the extension

Researchers often encounter molecular structure files (.pdb, .cif, .gro, etc.) while browsing repositories, datasets, or institutional storage. Downloading these files manually just to visualize them in a separate tool disrupts workflows and slows discovery.

Mol* Linker eliminates this friction. It automatically detects structure files on supported sites and injects a one-click badge to open them directly in the *Mol** web viewer. No downloads, no context-switching. Just instant, interactive visualization.
