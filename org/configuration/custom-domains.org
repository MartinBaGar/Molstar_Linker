#+title: Custom Domains
#+weight: 310

Mol* Linker supports private instances of GitLab, Nextcloud, and other platforms. To enable the extension on custom domains:

1. Visit the private instance hosting your file.
 #+ATTR_HTML: :width 70% :class center-image
  [[img:gitlab_instance.png]]

2. Open the Mol* Linker popup by clicking the extension icon in your browser toolbar.
 #+ATTR_HTML: :width 70% :class center-image
  [[img:gitlab_instance_auth_popup.png]]
   
3. Click on the "Authorize This Domain" button which will allow Mol* Linker the request authorizations for your instance.
 #+ATTR_HTML: :width 70% :class center-image
  [[img:gitlab_instance_perm_request.png]]
   
4. Allow the new permissions request.
 #+ATTR_HTML: :width 70% :class center-image
 [[img:gitlab_instance_badge_tab.png]]
   
You should now be able to see a badge appear.
